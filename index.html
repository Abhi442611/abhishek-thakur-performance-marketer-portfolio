About

WhoI Am

Hi, I’m Abhishek. I help brands grow by making their marketing work harder and smarter. My work sits across performance marketing and growth marketing. I run SEO, Google Ads, and Meta Ads campaigns, and I fix the websites and landing pages behind them so more visitors become real leads and customers. What I enjoy most is the detail behind the numbers. I look at where people click, where they drop off, and what makes them act. Then I cut what isn’t working and put more into what is. Done consistently, that brings down the cost of getting a customer and raises the return on every rupee spent. I’m comfortable working with teams across design, sales, and leadership, and I keep my reports simple so everyone can see what’s working and why. Whether the goal is more leads, better ROI, or steady long-term growth, I focus on results you can measure. If you’re looking for someone who treats your marketing budget like their own, let’s talk. Short version for your headline Performance & Growth Marketing Specialist | SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads | Helping brands grow with data and clear strategy I dropped most of the tool names (GA4, GTM, Looker Studio) to keep it natural. What is this Free Backlink Finder Tool?

This is a free backlink building tool built by Abhishek Thakur, SEO Expert. It matches your website and niche against a curated vault of 1,143 free backlink opportunities: guest post sites, high-DA directories, forums, Q&A communities, business profiles, Web 2.0 publishing platforms, and press and review sites — then turns the results into a prioritised action plan.

Unlike scraped lists, every source here comes with step-by-step instructions, a pro tip, an estimated Domain Authority and a priority score — so a junior SEO executive can execute it like a senior one.

Yes. There's no sign-up, no paywall and no credit card required. Every source in the vault is free to submit to — none of the links in the results are paid placements.

The vault only includes legitimate, hand-verified sources — no PBNs or link farms — so the links follow Google's current guidelines. As always, relevance and natural anchor text matter more than volume.

Priority blends estimated Domain Authority, niche relevance to what you entered, and how easy the source is to get accepted on — so the top of your list is the best return for the least effort.

DA is refreshed periodically rather than in real time, so it's worth a quick spot-check with your usual SEO tool before a big outreach push, especially for sources you plan to prioritise.
